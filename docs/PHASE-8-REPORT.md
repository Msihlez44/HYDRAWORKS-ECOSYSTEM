# Phase 8 — HYDRAHOST

HYDRAHOST provides configurable Business Starter, Pro Business and Premium Business plans; domain registration requests; website and email hosting subscriptions; mailboxes; invoices; client status views; and administrator fulfilment controls.

Initial monthly prices are R99, R199 and R349. They are stored in the database and remain administrator-editable. Automated provisioning uses an adapter boundary. Without registrar/cPanel credentials, jobs remain visibly pending for administrator fulfilment and no production success is fabricated.
