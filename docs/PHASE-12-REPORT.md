# Phase 12 — Support and Disputes

Phase 12 adds a transaction-aware support centre for the complete HYDRA WORKS ecosystem.

## Delivered

- Nine support categories and priority-based SLA deadlines.
- Tickets and disputes linked to authorised operational transactions.
- Evidence uploads with restricted file types and 10 MB limits.
- Automatic case conversations containing the requester, transaction parties, and support staff.
- Staff queues with status, category, priority, assignee, and overdue filters.
- Assignment, financial-hold, resolution, reopening, and closure workflows.
- A unified admin case view with parties, evidence, messages, payment records, operational context, and chronological actions.
- In-app support notifications and immutable audit-log entries for administrative actions.

## Lifecycle

`OPEN → ASSIGNED → WAITING → RESOLVED → CLOSED`

Resolved cases can be returned to `ASSIGNED`; closed cases are final. Financial holds are recorded as administrative controls and do not directly mutate external payment-provider state.
